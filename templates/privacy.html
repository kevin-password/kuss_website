Privacy Policy

How we protect your information

1. Information We Collect

When you register as a KUSS member, we collect:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, registration number
  • Academic Information: Year of study, program, membership type
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, bio (optional)
  • Financial Information: Payment records (accessible only to Treasurer)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Manage your membership and provide access to member portal
  • Send important notifications about events and activities
  • Process membership fee payments
  • Facilitate communication between members and leadership

3. Information Sharing

Public Information: Your name, email, and profile picture are visible to all KUSS members.

Private Information: Your phone number is only visible to logged-in KUSS members. It is never shared with external parties.

Financial Information: Payment records are only accessible to the Treasurer.

4. Data Security

  • Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text
  • Phone numbers are hidden from public view and search engines
  • Access to sensitive data is restricted to authorized leaders only
  • HTTPS encryption protects all data transmission

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data through your member portal
  • Update your profile information at any time
  • Request deletion of your account by contacting the General Secretary
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

6. Contact Us
