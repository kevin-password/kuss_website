Who We Are

Building the future of surgery from Kabale.

The Kabale University Surgical Society is a student-led platform dedicated to advancing surgical education, research, mentorship, and professional development.

Through case presentations, skills sessions, research activities, mentorship, and academic collaboration, KUSS creates opportunities for medical students to engage with surgery beyond the traditional classroom.

“Above and beyond — in learning, service, research, and surgical excellence.”
