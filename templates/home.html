Who We Are

Above and beyond the ordinary.

The Kabale University Surgical Society provides a platform for medical students who aspire to explore, understand and contribute to the field of surgery.

Through structured learning, mentorship, research, surgical skills training and professional engagement, KUSS helps members develop the knowledge, confidence and character required for a future in surgical practice.

Our Mission

To equip society members with the knowledge, skills, mentorship and opportunities necessary to pursue successful careers in surgery while fostering excellence, professionalism and lifelong learning.

What We Do

Building the complete future surgeon.

KUSS brings together education, research, mentorship and professional development around one shared surgical vision.

01

Surgical Education

Case discussions, journal clubs, surgical skills sessions, clinical learning and exposure to different surgical specialties.

02

Research

Encouraging students to develop research skills, ask clinically meaningful questions and contribute to evidence-based surgical practice.

03

Mentorship

Creating meaningful connections between students, residents, specialists and experienced surgical professionals.
