Who We Are

Building the future of surgery from Kabale.

The Kabale University Surgical Society is a student-led platform dedicated to advancing surgical education, research, mentorship, and professional development.

We create opportunities for medical students to engage with surgery beyond the traditional classroom through case discussions, skills sessions, research activities, mentorship, and meaningful academic collaboration.

“Above and beyond — in learning, service, research, and surgical excellence.”

What We Do

The pillars of KUSS

A platform designed to complement medical education with practical exposure, academic curiosity, and professional mentorship.

01

Surgical Education

Hands-on workshops, seminars, case presentations, surgical skills sessions, and academic discussions designed to deepen clinical understanding.

02

Research & Innovation

Fostering a culture of inquiry through journal clubs, research activities, academic collaboration, and student-led projects.

03

Mentorship & Advocacy

Connecting students with surgical professionals while encouraging professional development, leadership, and advocacy within the medical community.
