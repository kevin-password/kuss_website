Who we are
More than a society.
A community preparing for the future of surgery.

The Kabale University Surgical Society brings together students with an interest in surgery and related disciplines to learn, collaborate and grow.

Through structured educational activities, clinical exposure, research opportunities and mentorship, KUSS creates an environment where students can begin developing the knowledge, skills and professional values required of future surgeons.

Learn more about KUSS
Our foundation

Three pillars. One direction.

Everything we do is designed to move students closer to becoming capable, ethical and compassionate clinicians.

01 — EDUCATION

Learn deeply.

Clinical discussions, case-based learning, journal clubs and surgical skills activities that connect classroom knowledge with practice.

Our vision
“To achieve a society that nurtures future holistic surgeons.”

Surgery demands more than technical competence. It requires judgement, communication, integrity, teamwork, resilience and a commitment to patients.

KUSS seeks to cultivate these qualities from the earliest stages of medical training, giving students opportunities to learn beyond the conventional curriculum.
